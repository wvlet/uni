# SVG version of the Uni logo

## Goal

A scalable `docs/public/uni-logo.svg` that matches `uni-logo-1024x1024.png` (a Gemini-generated
raster), so the logo stays sharp at any size.

## Approach

Automatic tracing (potrace, vtracer) reproduces the pixels but not the design: gradients become
blotchy colour bands and outlines wobble. The SVG is instead a model of how the logo is built:

- **Ribbons and blades.** Every component is a ribbon or blade stacked back to front: four mane
  locks, the neck ribbon, the gray neck crescent and throat blade, cheek, face, ear, horn, and the
  U's four ribbons. A front ribbon's edge is the only border between two colours, so there are no
  gaps or slivers between neighbours.
- **One cubic per boundary.** Each boundary line is a single cubic Bézier (at most two inflection
  points) that runs from junction to junction, where a junction is a colour boundary or a blade
  tip. Mane locks are three cubics sharing a tip (upper edge, lower edge, crease). Outlines are
  split at their corners and at the points where interior boundaries meet them.
- **Even white border.** The white gap around the two front components (head and U) is a band of
  constant width along their outlines. It is an SVG mask, so the gap stays transparent on any
  background.
- **Fills.** Each face is a two-stop linear gradient fitted to the original pixels inside it.

## Status (snapshot)

- U outline: straight sides plus four single cubics, all within 1.6px of the original.
- Head outline: most spans are single cubics within 2.3px. Three spans (lower S tail,
  chin to muzzle, muzzle tip to forehead) still need their missing junctions located; until then
  they are drawn as a few cubics with matching tangents at the joins.
